Privacy Policy

Updated October 31, 2026 · Русский

Welcome to GPS Speedometer! This Privacy Policy explains how letqq ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the GPS Speedometer mobile application (the "Service"). The app has no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising. Your trips, routes, and settings stay on your device unless you export or share them. A few optional features contact other services with coordinates, and the optional one-time purchase involves a license server run by the developer and a payment provider.

1. Information We Process

a) Location Data (Precise)

Purpose: The core of the Service is your device's precise location. We use it to calculate speed, distance, and route while a trip is running, to show your position on the map, and, if those features are on, to fetch weather and speed limits.

Processing & Sharing:

  • Local use: speed, distance, and routes are calculated on your device. Location comes from Android, including the fused location provider of Google Play services where available; that is a system component and is not run by this app.
  • External services: only the coordinates described in sections 1(e) and 1(f) can leave your device, and only when the matching feature is on. Trip routes are never sent to any server.

Collection Trigger: Location is accessed while a trip is being recorded or the app is open. Recording uses a foreground service, indicated by a persistent notification. If a Weather widget is on your home screen, it also refreshes about every 30 minutes in the background using your last known location. We do not upload your location history; trips are stored only on your device.

b) Sensor Data

Types: Accelerometer, magnetometer, and rotation vector sensors.

Purpose: To calculate the compass heading.

Processing: This data is processed entirely on your device. It is not stored on external servers or transmitted to us.

c) Trip History

Purpose: To let you review past trips, replay them, and see statistics.

Processing: Recorded trips, waypoints, and map pins are stored in a local database on your device. They never leave your device unless you export or share them yourself, for example as GPX files, a full backup file, or a shared trip image. Importing a GPX file reads it on the device only.

d) Heart Rate & Calories (Optional)

Purpose: If you enable the heart rate feature in Settings, the app can read heart rate, active and total calories, basal metabolic rate, and workout sessions from Health Connect.

Processing: This data comes from your watch or fitness band and is stored only with the related trip on your device. The app never sends it anywhere. You can disable the feature or revoke the permissions at any time.

e) Network Access & Third-Party Data

Purpose: Internet access is used for the map, weather, speed limits, and the optional license check. The app makes the following requests, and no others:

  • Map tiles and fonts - requested from the VersaTiles public tile service. If you choose the satellite style, imagery is requested from Esri's ArcGIS World Imagery service. These requests contain only the geographic area visible on the map and carry no user or device identifiers. Tiles may be cached on the device.
  • Weather - when the weather feature or widget is on, your coordinates, rounded to four decimal places (about 10 meters), are sent to Open-Meteo, or to MET Norway as a fallback. The request contains only latitude and longitude. Both services are free, open, and need no API key or account.
  • Speed limits - when the speed limit sign is on, approximate coordinates, rounded to a grid of about 110 meters, are sent to a public OpenStreetMap data service (Overpass API, with overpass.private.coffee and maps.mail.ru as fallbacks). Results are cached on the device for a few minutes. The sign is off by default.
  • License - only after you enter a license key. See section 1(f).

All of these services are operated by third parties and can see your IP address like any web server. Links you tap, such as the purchase page or this policy, open in your browser.

Place names: To show a place name for a trip or for the weather, the app may use Android's built-in geocoder. On most devices this is a system service that can send the coordinates to the device manufacturer's or Google's location service. It is not run by this app, and it is used only when you open a trip or the weather details.

f) Purchase and License (GPS Speedometer+)

Purpose: GPS Speedometer+ is an optional one-time purchase. The app never asks for your email address. The purchase happens on a web page at apps.letqq.mnogod.ru that opens in your browser. It is run by the developer on a server located in Russia. Seller details are on the apps.letqq.mnogod.ru/contacts page.

Data Processed on the Purchase Page:

  • Email. You enter it on the purchase page. It is used for the payment receipt, to deliver your license key, and for support, and it is passed to YooKassa, the payment provider.
  • Payment. Payments are processed by YooKassa. You enter card or other payment details on YooKassa's pages, and they never reach the app or our server.
  • Stored on our server. Your email, the order amount, status and dates, the YooKassa payment ID, and your license key, which is kept encrypted. While an order is pending, the server also keeps a keyed hash of your IP address to limit abuse; it is cleared once the order is no longer pending. Raw IP addresses are not stored, and server logs do not contain emails or IP addresses.
  • Cookie. The purchase pages remember the colors of your app theme in a cookie for 30 days, so they match the app.

Data Sent by the App:

  • Entering a key. The app sends the key and a device identifier to the license server. The identifier is a SHA-256 hash of your Android ID and the system build fingerprint, and the raw values are never sent. The server stores it to tie the key to your device and replies with a signed token, which the app keeps encrypted on your device.
  • Re-checking. About once a week, while the app is in use, it re-validates the token by sending the token and the device identifier.
  • If you have not entered a key, the app never contacts the license server.

Local Storage: The license token is stored encrypted on your device. The app unlocks features offline using it.

g) Crash Logs and Feedback

Crash log: If the app crashes, an error log with the app version, device model, Android version, and the technical error is saved on the device and shown to you on the next launch. It is sent nowhere unless you choose to share it.

Feedback: Sending feedback is voluntary. It opens your email app with a draft to letqqe@gmail.com, and we receive only what you choose to send.

2. Permissions

The app requests the following permissions. Some are required for core functionality, while others are optional and used only by the feature named:

ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION
Speed, distance, and route while a trip is running
ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION
Fallback when fine location is unavailable
FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION
Keep trip recording alive in background
POST_NOTIFICATIONS
Trip-in-progress notification (Android 13+)
POST_PROMOTED_NOTIFICATIONS
Optional, show trip status as a promoted live notification (Android 16+)
INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE
Map tiles, weather, speed limit and license requests (see Network Use)
SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW
Optional floating speed gauge
FLASHLIGHT
Optional, flashlight control from the app
WAKE_LOCK, VIBRATE
Keep device awake during tracking; vibrate on speed warnings
REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS
Optional, prevents system from killing long trips
health.READ_HEART_RATE
Optional, heart rate from Health Connect
health.READ_ACTIVE_CALORIES_BURNED, health.READ_TOTAL_CALORIES_BURNED, health.READ_BASAL_METABOLIC_RATE
Optional, calories from Health Connect
health.READ_EXERCISE
Optional, workout sessions from Health Connect, used together with heart rate and calories

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with advertisers. To provide the features of the app, specific data is shared with the following service providers, only when you use the matching feature:

  • Weather providers (Open-Meteo, MET Norway): coordinates, to return local conditions.
  • Speed limit services (Overpass API servers): approximate coordinates, to return posted limits.
  • Map providers (VersaTiles, Esri): the visible map area, to return tiles.
  • YooKassa: your email and payment, to process the purchase and issue the receipt.
  • Android and Google Play services: location and place names, as described in section 1.
  • Legal requirements: we may disclose information if required by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

4. Data Security, Retention, and Deletion

Security: All network requests use HTTPS. The license token is stored encrypted on the device, and license keys are stored encrypted on the server. This page contains no analytics or third-party scripts; when opened from the app it keeps the app's theme colors in your browser's local storage for 30 days.

Retention:

  • Trips, waypoints, map pins, and heart rate samples: local database in app-private storage.
  • Settings: local Android DataStore.
  • Last location used by the weather widget: on the device.
  • Purchase records: on our server, kept as long as needed for support and accounting.

Backup: Android auto-backup may include trips, waypoints, and settings if you have it enabled. The license token, widget data, and crash log are excluded. Google's backup is end-to-end encrypted.

Deletion:

  • Single trip: open it in History and delete it.
  • Trips, waypoints, tags, and settings: Settings > Data > Select data to delete.
  • Everything on the device: uninstall the app.
  • Heart rate data: turn the feature off in Settings; existing samples are deleted with their trip.
  • Purchase data on the server: write to letqqe@gmail.com. Records that must be kept for accounting may be retained.

5. Speed & Safety Disclaimer

GPS Speedometer is a software tool that relies on your device's GPS and on third-party map data.

  • Approximate readings: speed, distance, and speed limit information can be inaccurate or delayed, especially with a weak GPS signal. Always follow posted signs and your vehicle's own instruments.
  • Stay attentive: do not operate the app while driving where it could distract you.

6. Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for users 18 and older and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect data from children. There are no accounts, no messaging, and no content shared with other users. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us.

7. No Tracking or Profiling

GPS Speedometer does not include:

  • advertising identifiers or ad SDKs
  • analytics, telemetry, or third-party crash reporting
  • behavioral profiling or targeted advertising
  • cross-app tracking
  • access to your contacts, photos, microphone, or camera

Most data used by the app is processed on the device and stays there.
