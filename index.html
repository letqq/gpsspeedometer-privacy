Privacy Policy

Updated October 7, 2026 · Русский

This policy describes how GPS Speedometer handles your data. The app has no accounts and no servers of its own, and it does not sell personal information. Your data stays on your device unless you export it. A few optional features contact public third-party services with approximate coordinates; these are listed in the sections below, and each can be turned off.

Location

Location is used to calculate speed, distance, and route while a trip is running. While the app is open it is also used to show your position on the map and, if those features are on, to look up weather and speed limits (see below). Trip routes are saved locally with the trip record and are never sent to any server. Only the approximate coordinates described in the Weather, Speed Limit Sign, and Map sections leave your device.

Weather

When the weather feature is enabled, the app sends your current coordinates to a weather service to fetch local conditions. The request contains only latitude and longitude - no identifiers, no trip data, no personal information. The app uses Open-Meteo as the primary provider and MET Norway as a fallback. Both are free, open, and do not require an API key or user account. You can disable the weather feature at any time in Settings.

Speed Limit Sign

When the speed limit sign is enabled, the app asks a public OpenStreetMap data service (Overpass API, with overpass.private.coffee and maps.mail.ru as fallbacks) for the posted speed limit of nearby roads. The request contains only your approximate coordinates, rounded to a grid of about 100 meters. It contains no identifiers and no trip data. Results are cached on the device to keep requests few. These services are operated by third parties and can see your IP address like any web server. You can turn the sign off at any time in Settings.

Place Names

To show a place name for a trip, the app may use Android's built-in geocoder. On most devices this is a system service that can send the coordinates to the device manufacturer's or Google's location service. It is not run by this app, and it is used only when you view a trip.

Heart Rate & Calories

If you enable the heart rate feature in Settings, the app can read heart rate and calorie data from Health Connect. This data comes from your watch or fitness band and is stored only with the related trip on your device. You can disable this feature or revoke the permission at any time.

Trip History

Recorded trips are stored in a local database on your device. Trips never leave your device unless you export them yourself as GPX files.

Settings

Your settings and preferences are stored locally using Android DataStore. They may be included in Android's automatic backup if you have that enabled. The app does not transmit them.

What the App Does Not Do

  • No accounts, email addresses, or phone numbers
  • No advertising identifiers or ad SDKs
  • No analytics, telemetry, or third-party crash reporting
  • No third-party SDKs that send personal data

Network Use

The app makes the following network requests, and no others:

  • Map tiles - requested from the VersaTiles public tile service. These requests contain only the geographic area visible on the map. They do not include any user or device identifiers.
  • Satellite imagery - if you choose the satellite map style, tiles are requested from Esri's ArcGIS World Imagery service, again only for the visible area.
  • Weather data - when the weather feature is on, the app requests current conditions from Open-Meteo or MET Norway using your coordinates. See the Weather section above.
  • Speed limits - when the speed limit sign is on, the app queries Overpass API servers with approximate coordinates. See the Speed Limit Sign section above.

Data Storage

  • Trips, waypoints, and map pins: local database (app-private storage)
  • Settings: local Android DataStore
  • Heart rate samples: stored with the related trip in the local database

Android auto-backup may include this data if you have it enabled. Google's backup is end-to-end encrypted.

Deleting Your Data

  • Single trip: open it in History and delete it
  • All trips: Settings > Data > Delete all trips
  • All map pins: Settings > Data > Delete all waypoints
  • Everything: Settings > Data > Press on delete all trips, delete all waypoints, reseat all settings (if you want), or just simply uninstall the app
  • Heart rate data: turn the feature off in Settings; existing samples are deleted with their trip

Permissions

ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION
Speed, distance, and route while a trip is running
ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION
Fallback when fine location is unavailable
FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION
Keep trip recording alive in background
POST_NOTIFICATIONS
Trip-in-progress notification (Android 13+)
POST_PROMOTED_NOTIFICATIONS
Optional, show trip status as a promoted live notification (Android 16+)
INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE
Map tiles, weather, and speed limit requests (see Network Use)
SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW
Optional floating speed gauge
FLASHLIGHT
Optional, flashlight control from the app
WAKE_LOCK, VIBRATE
Keep device awake during tracking; vibrate on speed warnings
REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS
Optional, prevents system from killing long trips
health.READ_HEART_RATE + related
Optional, read heart rate and calories via Health Connect

Children

This app is intended for users 18 and older. It does not collect data from anyone, including children. There are no accounts, no messaging, and no content shared with other users.
